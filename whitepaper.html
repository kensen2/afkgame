One dungeon, one coin, a fixed daily pool

AFK Dungeon is a browser idle-battler: your hero fights wave after wave on its own, even while you are away. Every day the realm releases a fixed supply of $DGN, split between all players by how strong their dungeon is. This paper explains where the coin comes from, where it goes, and what the numbers look like for 100 to 2,000 players.

1The game

Two heroes, the Blue-Gold Warrior (tank) and the Lion Blade (damage), push through 100 waves of skeletons and fallen adventurers across four dungeon floors. Every 5th wave is elite, every 10th is a boss, and wave 100 is Malakor, Lord of the Dungeon.

Combat is automatic. You decide what to upgrade, which skills to raise and when to spend. Death is not a loss: you restart one wave back with everything you earned. While the game is closed your hero keeps farming at a reduced offline rate.

4The daily pool

Each realm day the pool is refilled and split between every active player. It is not first-come: the whole day's pool is divided by weight, so everyone who played that day gets a share.

pool(day) = 2,000,000 + 30% × $DGN spent in game yesterday your share = pool × your counted DP ÷ everyone's counted DP

The base comes from the season reserve. The second part grows with activity: when players spend more, tomorrow's pool is bigger. That softens the main weakness of a purely fixed pool, where every new player makes everyone else's share smaller.

Your share builds up hour by hour, including while the game is closed, for up to 24 hours. Come back once a day and nothing is lost.

5Dungeon Power & the Vault

Your weight in the pool is your Dungeon Power (DP). It has two parts:

DP = season best wave × 1 + account level × 0.5 + relic DP

Progress DP rewards playing. Relic DP is bought with $DGN and lasts the season. A free player who reaches wave 90 has roughly 200 DP. A single Bone Idol adds 100.

The Vault caps what counts

Your DP only counts up to a multiple of the realm's average DP. The Vault sets that multiple. A huge collection of relics behind a small Vault counts as a small one, so the biggest wallets cannot take the whole pool.

7Taking it out

  • Your own deposit comes first. $DGN you deposited and have not spent can be withdrawn at any time, with no fee and no daily limit. It never touches the pool.
  • Pool earnings can be withdrawn once per realm day, up to that day's share, with a 5% fee. Whatever you do not take stays in the game, where you can spend it or take it on a later day.
  • New accounts wait 12 hours before their first pool withdrawal. The same rule applies to everyone.
  • Spending uses pool earnings first, so your deposit stays withdrawable for as long as possible.
  • The vault contract has a daily global cap and keeps only a few days of float, so a compromised key can never reach the whole treasury.

8Seasons

A season lasts 30 days. Relic DP, Vault levels and the Idle Pass belong to the season. When it ends, Dungeon Power resets, so a player who joins on day one of the next season starts on equal terms. Heroes, upgrades and account level stay.

Unspent $DGN, both deposited and earned, is never reset.

10Treasury & sustainability

The pool base (2,000,000 $DGN a day, about 60M per season) is paid from a season reserve set aside at launch. 40% of all in-game spending and the 5% withdrawal fee flow back to the treasury. In the simulation the reserve's net cost per season is about $4,400 to $5,400 at the launch price, depending on player count. Burns remove 10M to 40M $DGN per season.

The pool base will be reviewed each season and published before the season starts. If the realm is small, the base is lowered so players do not sell more than they bring in.

12Risks

  • This is a game, not an investment. Nothing here promises a return. You can lose what you deposit if the token price falls or the game closes.
  • The pool depends on new activity. Payouts come from the season reserve and from other players' spending. If fewer people join or spend, shares shrink and the token price is likely to fall.
  • Late joiners earn less. Shares are highest early in a season and early in the game's life.
  • Rules can change between seasons. Pool size, splits and prices may be adjusted and will be announced before a season starts.
  • Regulation. Crypto payments and token rewards are regulated differently in each country and may be restricted where you live. Check your local rules before depositing.
  • Smart-contract and key risk. Contracts can have bugs. The daily cap and limited float reduce, but do not remove, that risk.
