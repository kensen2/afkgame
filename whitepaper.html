Fight deeper, produce more

AFK Dungeon is a browser idle-battler. Your hero clears wave after wave on their own, and every wave you reach raises how much $DGN your dungeon produces each hour. Waves 1–20 are free. Keys open the sealed gates beyond. Seasons last 10 days.

1The game

Two heroes, the Blue-Gold Warrior and the Lion Blade, push through 100 waves across ten dungeon floors, from the Bone Crypt to Malakor's Throne. Every 5th wave is elite, every 10th is a boss. Enemies drop gold, and gold buys upgrades and skills. Combat is automatic, and your dungeon keeps producing for up to 12 hours while the game is closed.

2Hourly production

Your production rate is set by the highest wave you have cleared this season. It is shown live in the top-right corner and builds up second by second. Press Claim to move it into your in-game balance.

3Free play: waves 1–20

Anyone can play without paying. Gold upgrades take a free player to wave 20, where production reaches its free cap of 2,000 DGN an hour. After the wave 20 boss the Sealed Gate appears for ten seconds, then the hero returns to wave 15 and keeps looping 15–20. Free players take their share from the free pool every day.

4Keys and sealed gates

Every wave after 20 needs one key. A key is used automatically the first time you enter a wave, and that wave stays open for the rest of the season. Keys come in packs of ten, one tier for every ten waves. Prices are set in USD and converted at the current DGN price. Unused keys carry over to the next season.

5The two daily pools

Every day the realm releases two fixed pools: 10,000,000 DGN for free players and 10,000,000 DGN for players who deposited this season. Each pool is split by production rate, so a stronger dungeon takes a bigger share. The pool is fixed: when more lords join, every share gets smaller. Your share is capped at your own production, and whatever is not handed out is burned.

your share (DGN / day) = pool × your production ÷ everyone's production never more than your own production

For players who deposited, pool earnings start 48 hours after the first deposit of the season. During those two days their production pays back what they deposited.

6Taking DGN out

  • What you deposited comes first. Up to the amount you deposited this season can leave without the daily limit, as your production refills your balance.
  • Beyond that, today's pool share. Even if more has built up in your balance, only today's share can leave. The rest stays in the game for keys, the Forge and events.
  • Two steps. First move DGN from the game into the vault, where the 5% fee is taken (half of it is burned). Then send it from the vault to your wallet.
  • Holding rule. Your wallet must have held at least 20,000 DGN for the last 12 hours. This stops lords from farming the pool and leaving the same hour.

7Forge and store

The Forge multiplies your hero's damage and health by ×1.10 per level and is paid from your in-game balance. Keys open waves; the Forge makes you strong enough to clear them. The store sells Time Skip, Gold Rush, Idle Pass, Skill Tomes and Revive, also paid in DGN.

8Seasons

A season lasts 10 days. When it ends, waves, gold, levels, upgrades, the Forge and the DGN produced inside the game are reset, so everyone starts the next season on equal terms. Unused keys carry over, and any part of your deposit you have not yet taken out stays yours.

10Burns and transparency

The game shows two separate numbers. Burned on-chain counts only real burns you can verify on the Robinhood Chain explorer: half of every withdrawal fee and every pool share left undistributed. Spent in the dungeon counts DGN spent inside the game on keys, the Forge and the store; it is removed from the game but is not an on-chain burn.

The team earns from trading fees on the DGN market and does not trade the token itself. The vault, fee and burn addresses will be published here at launch.

11Roadmap

Phase 1now

In the browser, test mode. Production, keys, sealed gates, the Forge, the two-step withdrawal and seasons run against a demo realm with demo tokens.

Phase 2

Server and wallets. Sign in with an EVM wallet. Balances, production and the daily pools move to the server. Live DGN price, holding-rule checks and daily on-chain burns.

Phase 3

Token launch on Robinhood Chain. Real deposits, vault contract with a daily cap, withdrawals to wallets, in-game buy button. Security review first.

Phase 4

Events and growth. Mid-season events for built-up balances, clans, referrals, leaderboards and the Lion Blade NFT hero.

12Risks

  • This is a game, not an investment. Nothing here promises a return. The token price can fall and you can lose what you put in.
  • Shares shrink as the realm grows. Pools are fixed; more players means less for each.
  • Rules can change between seasons. Pool sizes, key prices and rates may be adjusted and will be announced before a season starts.
  • Regulation. Crypto payments and token rewards are treated differently in each country. Check the rules where you live.
  • Contract and key risk. Smart contracts can have bugs. A daily cap and a limited vault float reduce, but do not remove, that risk.
